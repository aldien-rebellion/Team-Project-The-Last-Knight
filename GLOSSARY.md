# Domain glossary

| Term | Meaning in this project |
| --- | --- |
| Merchant trade | One purchase or sale, coordinating Gold, received or removed items, limited stock, and progression of the random merchant offer. A failed trade must not charge Gold, remove items, decrease stock, or advance the offer. |
| Quick Item consumption | Using the selected quick-slot item, including selection, eligibility, captured item identity, completion, and cancellation. Only successful completion consumes one item. |
| Temporary buff | A timed player effect. Reapplying refreshes its duration instead of stacking another instance. Gameplay and the active-buff display observe the same lifecycle. |
